oak.coop is an experiment in grounding the web.

we ask: where is oakland online and where could it be? how might we rewild the internet with local roots? can we connect and protect a native digital habitat on the open social web? what would our local station of the pbs of the internet look like?

we have tools and inspiration to better care for local knowledge, data, and attention in the east bay. so let's start.
